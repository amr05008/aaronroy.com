      Before you trust these numbers

        How this was made, and what it can't see

        The line is Aaron's bike computer's 1 Hz GPS trace from the Open Cat 4/5 race. Four passes of the lap were averaged into a single centerline and resampled every metre; 87% of the lap has all four passes. Race speed and the lap time are his second lap.

        The ground is USGS 3DEP lidar at about 1 m resolution. Gradient and side slope are measured on that surface, across a 3 m-wide strip around the line.

        Limits. GPS can sit 2 to 3 m off the real line, which matters on a hillside: the 57% side slope along the dam embankment at about 200 m is probably the trace sitting on the bank, not a line anyone rode. Lidar is bare ground from an earlier survey, so barriers, stairs, sand, ruts and tape are not here. The time gaps have their own limits, listed under the table.
