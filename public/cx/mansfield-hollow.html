      How this was made, and what it can't see

      The line comes from a GPX export of the Strava segment "Mansfield Hollow 2024 Lap". The file holds seven passes of the lap, which were folded into one centerline (they agree to about half a metre) and resampled every metre.

      The ground is USGS 3DEP lidar at about 1 m resolution. Gradient and side slope are measured on that surface, across a 3 m-wide strip around the line.

      Limits. This is the 2024 course, so this year's taping may differ, and the lap starts where the file starts, which may not be the race start line. GPS can sit a few metres off the real line: the 44% side slope beside the dam is probably the trace drifting onto the embankment. Lidar is bare ground from an earlier survey, so barriers, stairs, sand, ruts and tape are not here.
