          Before you trust these numbers

            How this was made, and what it can't see

            The line comes from a GPX export of one ride that matched the Strava segment "ss_2026_preview". The file holds seven laps, which were folded into one centerline and resampled every metre. The export has a point about every 6 m and the laps agree to about 1.6 m.

            The ground is USGS 3DEP lidar at about 1 m resolution. Gradient and side slope are measured on that surface, across a 3 m-wide strip around the line.

            Limits. This is a preview ride, so race-day taping may differ. The lap starts where that ride ended, which may not be the race start line. The file is coarse and GPS can sit a few metres off the real line, so short features are smoothed over and the side-slope numbers on banks are approximate. Where two taped lanes run side by side the trace can blur them together. Lidar is bare ground from an earlier survey, so barriers, stairs, sand, ruts, logs and tape are not here.
