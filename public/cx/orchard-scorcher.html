            How this was made, and what it can't see

            The line comes from a GPX export of the course builder's setup ride on 23 Nov 2026, six days before the race. It is the last lap of that ride, a single pass with a point about every 4 m, resampled every metre. The export has no timestamps, so there is no race speed or lap time.

            The ground is USGS 3DEP lidar at about 1 m resolution. Gradient and side slope are measured on that surface, across a 3 m-wide strip around the line.

            Limits. This is one pass from a ride made while the course was still being adjusted, so race-day taping may differ. Earlier in the same ride the first lap took an extra switchback in the north-west corner that the last lap skipped; this page follows the last lap. The lap starts at an arbitrary point near where the ride began, not at the race start line. With a single pass there is no averaging, and GPS can sit 2 to 3 m off the real line, which matters on a hillside: the 26% side slope at about 280 m is on the bank in the north-west corner and is approximate. Where two taped lanes run side by side the trace can blur them together. Lidar is bare ground from an earlier survey, so barriers, stairs, sand, ruts, mud and tape are not here.
