      How this was made, and what it can't see

      The line is one rider's 1 Hz GPS trace from the 2025 Cat 4/5 race. Four laps were averaged into a single centerline and resampled every metre. Lap time and race speed are from the third lap, with speed worked out from the GPS positions.

      The ground is USGS 3DEP lidar at about 1 m resolution. Gradient and side slope are measured on that surface, across a 3 m-wide strip around the line.

      Limits. This is the 2025 course, so this year's taping may differ. The lap starts within about 10 m of where the race finished. GPS can sit 2 to 3 m off the real line, which matters on a hillside, and where two taped lanes run side by side the trace can blur them together. Lidar is bare ground from an earlier survey, so barriers, stairs, sand, ruts, mud and tape are not here. Race speed is one rider on one lap.
