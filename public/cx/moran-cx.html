      How this was made, and what it can't see

      The line is one bike computer's 1 Hz GPS trace. Three passes of the lap were averaged into a single centerline and resampled every metre.

      The ground is USGS 3DEP lidar at about 1 m resolution. Gradient and side slope are measured on that surface, across a 3 m-wide strip around the line.

      Limits. GPS can sit 2 to 3 m off the real line, which matters on a hillside. Lidar is bare ground from an earlier survey, so barriers, stairs, sand, ruts and tape are not here. Video links count from the race start at 0:22 in the video and are approximate.
