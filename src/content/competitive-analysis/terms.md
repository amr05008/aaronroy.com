# Competitive Analysis: Terms of Use

_Last updated: November 2, 2026_

Competitive Analysis is free, published by [Aaron Roy](https://aaronroy.com/about/), and its source is available under the MIT license at [github.com/amr05008/competitive-analysis](https://github.com/amr05008/competitive-analysis).

## What it is

A research method, carried out by the model in ChatGPT, Codex or Claude, plus a small server that looks up public records for it. The reports it produces are generated by the model from public web sources at the time you ask. They are a starting point for your own diligence, not a substitute for it.

## What it is not

Not investment, legal, or financial advice. Not a guarantee that any claim is accurate, even one labeled VERIFIED: the label means the model read a primary source, and models misread. Check anything you intend to act on against the linked source.

## The server

The plugin's four tools run on a server I pay for and run myself. It's free, it has daily limits, and it can be slow, rate-limited or unavailable; when it is, the teardown falls back to the app's own browsing. I may change the tools, limit them or shut the server down at any time. Its answers come from public sources at the moment you ask and can be incomplete or out of date, so the linked source is the record.

## Your responsibilities

- Use it to research companies and products, not people.
- Respect the terms of any research plugin you connect, and of the sites the model visits on your behalf.
- Don't rely on it for decisions where being wrong is expensive without verifying first.
- Don't use the server for bulk collection or load testing, or to get around a source's limits.
